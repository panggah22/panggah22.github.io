Our paper "Joint Peak Power and Carbon Emission Shaving in Active Distribution Systems Using Carbon Emission Flow-Based Deep Reinforcement Learning" has been accepted for publication in **Applied Energy**.

This work was led by Sangyoon Lee, with contributions from myself and Prof. Dae-Hyun Choi. The paper proposes a novel carbon emission flow-based deep reinforcement learning framework for jointly reducing peak power demand and carbon emissions in active distribution systems.
