Hi! I'm **Panggah Prabawa**, a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at [Chung-Ang University](https://www.cau.ac.kr/) in Seoul, South Korea. I work under the supervision of Prof. Dae-Hyun Choi in the Power Systems and Energy Optimization Laboratory.

## Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of **power systems optimization**, **renewable energy integration**, and **machine learning** for smart grid applications. I develop mathematical models and data-driven algorithms to address challenges in modern distribution networks.

**Key areas:**
- Volt-VAR optimization in active distribution systems
- Service restoration using multi-agent frameworks
- Deep reinforcement learning for energy management
- Electric vehicle charging coordination
- Carbon-aware power system scheduling
- Uncertainty quantification in renewable energy systems

## Education

- **Ph.D.** in Electrical Engineering, Chung-Ang University, Seoul, South Korea (ongoing)
- **M.S.** in Electrical Engineering, Chung-Ang University, Seoul, South Korea
- **B.S.** in Electrical Engineering

I have published in journals such as **IEEE Access**, **Applied Energy**, and **Energy Reports**, focusing on optimization frameworks that bridge the gap between theoretical research and practical grid operations.

Feel free to reach out if you're interested in my research or potential collaborations!
