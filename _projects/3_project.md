This research applies safe deep reinforcement learning techniques to energy management in active power distribution networks. The framework integrates hydrogen fueling stations as flexible loads and energy storage, ensuring operational constraints are never violated during exploration and deployment.

**Key Contributions:**

- Two-stage stochastic energy management framework
- Safety-constrained DRL policy optimization
- Hydrogen fueling station integration as flexible resources
- Guaranteed constraint satisfaction during learning

Published in **Applied Energy** (2024).
